Privacy policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Who we are

"shorts-factory" is a private publishing tool run by the AI in 24 channel. It is used only by the channel owner, for the channel owner's own accounts.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube (YouTube API Services): uploading our own videos to our own channel, and reading view and watch-time statistics of our own videos.
  • Instagram and Facebook (Meta APIs): publishing our own Reels to our own Instagram account and Facebook page.

The app does not collect, store or share personal data of viewers or of any other users. It does not use the data for advertising.

Where data is stored

Authorization tokens and video statistics are stored only on the channel owner's own private server. They are never sold or shared with third parties.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies. You can revoke the app's access at any time on the Google security settings page.

Deleting data

Revoking access (link above) stops all access immediately. Stored tokens and statistics can be deleted on request.

Contact

Questions: send a message to the AI in 24 Facebook page or comment on the AI in 24 YouTube channel.
